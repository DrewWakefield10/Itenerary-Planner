# Additional-place research — Palawan, Siargao, Davao

Review date: 21 October 2026. No original published directory files are edited. Acceptance verifies the named physical place and actual municipality, not price, opening, accessibility, availability, or safety. Durations and transport planning are estimates. Unknown pins remain null.

## Batch 1 — Palawan: 10 additional accepted places

Six editorial-surplus promotions retain existing stable IDs and original field-specific source dates: `el-nido-nagkalit-kalit-falls`, `puerto-princesa-iwahig-firefly-watching`, `puerto-princesa-ugong-rock`, `puerto-princesa-buenavista-viewdeck`, `puerto-princesa-bakers-hill`, and `puerto-princesa-balayong-peoples-park`. They were not in the original 200 accepted places. Their government sources remain in the JSON. Outlying falls, river, rock, and bay-viewpoint excursions remain manual full-block proposals, with hours, access, and guide arrangements to confirm. The 480-minute block is an excursion-day allocation, not time spent at the attraction. Iwahig requires an evening plan; no automatic daytime visit is proposed.

Four new venues: [Artcafe's restaurant page](https://elnidoboutiqueandartcafe.com/artcafe-restuarant/) establishes the restaurant, while [Philippine Primer](https://primer.com.ph/food/cuisines/fusion/artcafe/) supports its Serena Street / Buena Suerte / El Nido location; no historic prices or hours are copied. [Trattoria Altrove's branch directory](https://www.trattoriaaltrove.com/) gives distinct El Nido (Calle Hama, Masagana) and Coron (Rosario Street, Barangay 3) addresses. The El Nido Lounge is not counted again at the same address. Midnight-closing windows are not forced into the schema. [Kalye Artisano's description](https://kalyeartisano.com/pages/about-us) and [contact page](https://kalyeartisano.com/pages/contact) establish the artisan hub and Lio Tourism Estate location. The contact page's Google Maps destination link provides destination coordinates 11.2056236, 119.418869; the viewport center is not used.

Batch count: Palawan 10; Siargao 0; Davao 0; total 10. Work continues toward 38.

## Batch 2 — Siargao: 12 additional accepted places

Ten General Luna venues/places: Kermit Restaurant, Trattoria Altrove Siargao, Warung, Brunch Spot, Bravo Restaurant, NĀGA Siargao, Malinao Beach, Paseo de Cabuntog, The Hub by Lokal Lab (General Luna), and The Clubhouse Siargao. Their own venue websites support the six restaurants, community hub, and golf lounge; [Warung's Primer listing](https://primer.com.ph/food/location/philippines/siargao-island/warung-siargao/) supplies the municipality and address where the operator page omits them. The [SIPLAS plan](https://rsis.ramsar.org/RISapp/files/57292175/documents/PH2553_mgt230606.pdf) lists the Malinao beach area under General Luna, while [TIEZA's location record](https://tieza.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/SUMMARY-OF-BID-5-1.pdf) explicitly locates Paseo de Cabuntog in Poblacion I. Planned new facilities in municipal plans are not treated as open attractions.

[The Hub's official locations page](https://www.lokallab.org/the-hub) distinguishes its General Luna and Burgos branches. Only the General Luna venue is counted here; workshops, food counters, and tours within it are not counted as additional places. Its linked Google Maps destination point is 9.7827791, 126.1540921. [The Clubhouse's location guidance](https://theclubhouse.ph/location) explicitly makes bookings recommended and walk-ins subject to bay availability; this does not become a mandatory-booking flag or a confirmed reservation.

Two earlier editorial-surplus places are promoted with unchanged evidence dates: `siargao-somyot-cave` (Burgos) and `siargao-osmena-wetland` (Dapa). Both have null itinerary bases and manual scheduling because a suitable General Luna excursion route and current permitted visitor entry were not established. Their municipality is not inferred from the Siargao group. No new overnight base is created.

Kermit, Altrove Siargao, and Brunch Spot have the venue's published daily opening windows. Kermit's individual meal services differ within its general opening window; the booking note preserves that distinction. Other hours and all admission/meal prices remain unknown. New place clusters use stable IDs, except explicitly sourced Lio/Buena Suerte neighborhoods in batch 1, so a whole city or long Tourism Road does not become one assumed walking cluster.

Batch count: Palawan 10; Siargao 12; Davao 0; total 22. Work continues toward 38.

## Batch 3 — Davao: 16 additional accepted places

One editorial-surplus promotion, `davao-crocodile-park`, retains its government source fields and original checked dates. Two existing research-catalog IDs, `davao-bago-esplanade` and `davao-gulf-view-esplanade`, are reused rather than creating aliases for the same parks. The [city's 2024 green-space report](https://davaocity.gov.ph/sustainable-environment/city-to-open-more-green-spaces-parks/) distinguishes the two coastal parks, and records Toril Park, Matina Linear Park, Candelaria Park, and Communal Pocket Park as existing/opened sites. [The city park inventory](https://davaocity.gov.ph/environment/six-new-public-parks-to-open-in-davao-city/) identifies Davao City Botanical Park at Marfori Heights; Marfori Botanical Garden is an alias, not another place. Exact gates, opening windows, and current restrictions remain unknown. Each park has its own cluster.

The city tourism office supplies [GAP Farm's Ma-a address and hours](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/explore-the-city/attractions/man-made-nature-parks/gap-farm-resort/), [D’Leonor's Communal location and road access](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/explore-the-city/attractions/man-made-nature-parks/dleonor-inland-resort/), [D’ Japanese Tunnel's Matina Crossing address](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/explore-the-city/nightlife/spa/d-japanese-tunnel-family-resort-and-restaurant/), [Jack’s Ridge's Shrine Hills branch](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/explore-the-city/restaurants/restaurants/jacks-ridge-resort-and-restaurant-corp/), and [Marina Tuna's Sandawa Plaza branch](https://tourism.davaocity.gov.ph/explore-the-city/restaurants/restaurants/marina-tuna-2/). The [Department of Tourism's Davao brochure](https://itsmorefuninthephilippines.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/PDOTL_DavaoCamiguin_Web.pdf) establishes the historic Japanese Tunnel attraction; current tunnel access remains manual to confirm. GAP's internal displays and Jack’s Ridge's individual dining rooms are not counted separately. No promise of pool, ride, show, or tunnel availability is made.

[The diocesan newspaper's shrine listing](https://www.davaocatholicherald.com/mass-schedules/sto-nino-shrine-mass-schedule/) gives the Holy Infant Jesus of Prague shrine's Matina address; worship times are not copied as opening hours. The shrine and Jack’s Ridge use a source-backed Shrine Hills neighborhood cluster, not a whole-city cluster. [Alsons Development's account](https://alsonsdev.com/alsons-dev-launches-poblacion-market-central-the-newest-lifestyle-destination-in-davao-where-heritage-meets-hip/) establishes Poblacion Market Central; its C. Bangoy Street / Barangay 4A address is supported by the [Keppel branch record](https://www.keppelland.com.ph/pdf/CGFD_Keppel%20Philippines%20Properties%20Inc._SEC%2020-IS%20Y2023_13April2023_part3of3.pdf). The old closed Aldevinco Shopping Center and internal food hall are not counted again.

[DOST's 20 October 2026 article](https://dost.gov.ph/knowledge-resources/news/4560-from-micro-to-medium-dost-steps-up-assistance-to-apo-ni-lola-thrive-in-food-processing-business.html) confirms Apo ni Lola's Davao business. [The new-store report](https://mindanaotimes.com.ph/apo-ni-lola-durian-delicacies-opens-new-store-for-all-things-davao/) locates the 2025 pasalubong center in San Miguel Village, Matina. The house number is intentionally omitted: older government/manufacturer records give No. 28, while current store listings show No. 14. No factory tour or tasting is assumed.

Davao City is an independent highly urbanized city: province remains null for every Davao addition. Toril is an outlying district within Davao City, not another overnight base. Its park is manual so the traveler can review the longer road trip. D’Leonor and the Japanese Tunnel are also manual pending specific attraction access. All Davao pins remain null; no city-center coordinates substitute for venue locations.

## Exclusions and remaining gaps

[Davao Museum of History and Ethnography's own university announcement](https://international.addu.edu.ph/?p=12536) records its 2025 transfer to Ateneo de Davao, with a new museum building to house the collection. The [2026 university center list](https://www.addu.edu.ph/centers-and-institutes/) confirms its institution, but current visitor access/opening at the new venue was not established. It is excluded here rather than reusing its old Insular Village entrance or confusing it with Museo Dabawenyo or NMP-Davao.

Davao Bamboo Sanctuary was researched but excluded from this batch. The formerly relevant venue domain redirected to an unrelated gambling site during the live check; cached historical contact pages are not used as current supporting links. D’Leonor provides the supported inland recreation addition instead. Los Amigos Park was not selected because a useful excursion plan from the existing base was not established. Planned municipal museums, new rest-area facilities, package products, generic meal breaks, and workshop products are not additional physical places.

Somyot Cave and Osmeña Wetland remain directory-only with null base and catalog-stop link. All other additions map to existing overnight bases. Unknown rates are omitted, never zero; no admission prices were verified in this batch. Only Kermit, Altrove Siargao, Brunch Spot, and GAP Farm have sourced daily opening windows. All durations and unconfirmed transport advice are planning estimates. The two new pins are venue-linked destination points for Kalye Artisano and The Hub, not general town centers or map viewport centers.
