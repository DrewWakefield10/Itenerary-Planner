## Selection behavior

`food-profiles.json` has one primary counting category and independently supported coffee, meal, bakery, dessert and breakfast roles. Food markets remain distinct from individual restaurants. Exact café/coffee/food queries use these roles; combined destination/category/interest/municipality filters and pagination retain URL state. Café experience alone does not establish coffee service or meal suitability.

Generated sightseeing prioritizes non-food visits. A compatible named meal consumes a visit slot, replaces the flexible meal placeholder and shares the daily food allowance; it cannot append a fourth stop beyond the selected pace. Exact beach/culture filters do not introduce off-category restaurants. Ordinary days cap unrequested food visits; explicit food-focused requests can use the pace allowance.

AI receives bounded, category-balanced exact-ID menus with explicit meal/coffee roles. The application validates base, type, food balance, canonical duplicates/packages, geographic clusters, booking, known hours and pace, then rebuilds schedules from trusted facts. Invalid proposals are rejected transactionally. Unknown pins do not establish distances; unique clusters and manual entries deliberately retain open time rather than inventing a route. Saved itinerary snapshots are never regenerated from today’s catalog.
