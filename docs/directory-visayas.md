Total: 92 named-place research candidates; 74 accepted, 11 rejected, seven unresolved. The eight editorial-surplus replacements are genuine physical places, with identity and municipality evidence. Legacy meal/tour concepts are documented above but excluded from candidate counts. Alias/closed/proposed-venue candidates are retained transparently for deduplication or access research, not accepted coverage.

Eight accepted records retain sourced reference pins from 19 October 2026; 66 accepted pins remain unknown. Eleven accepted records have verified full or partial visitor addresses; 63 addresses remain unknown. A reference pin does not establish the entrance, accessible route, or current public access. Puka and Diniwid coordinates retain estimate status because they reference named DENR beach monitoring stations rather than entrances.

The province field follows geographic directory association for highly urbanized cities such as Cebu City and Iloilo City; it does not assert that these independent cities are governed by the corresponding province. Actual municipality/city is always stored separately. Boracay is a browsing group; the actual municipality is Malay and province is Aklan.

Validation checked unique IDs, exactly 74 accepted records, required verified identity and municipality evidence on every accepted entry, coherent coordinate pairs with source URLs, preserved coordinate review dates, no invented addresses, and no U+FFFD replacement characters in names. All eight replacements were checked against the before/after accepted array so the accepted selection stayed unchanged.

## Source review limits

Government, museum, venue-operator, and organizational primary sources were preferred. Public Waze listings corroborate the actual Panglao municipality for Shaka and Common Crew without adopting disputed street addresses or operating hours. Boracay place identities from the archival TPB map are combined with the island administrative reference; the map is explicitly an April 2020 tourist reference, not a current access or boundary survey. Some official PDFs returned 403/404 or timeouts; their indexed text was reviewed and supports only the named static facts reflected in metadata. Current access, reservations, practical costs, and schedules remain separate unknowns.
