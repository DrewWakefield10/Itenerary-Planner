Reviewed 21 October 2026. Only named physical places with sourced identity and actual city/municipality enter accepted coverage. Browsing groups are presentation labels, not overnight routing bases. Unknown addresses and pins stay null. Previous coordinate evidence retains its original check date.

## Batch save log

- Batch 4: 23 candidates appended: 19 Iloilo (15 accepted, three rejected, one unresolved) and four Cebu audit exclusions (two rejected, two unresolved). Iloilo accepted coverage includes actual Miagao, San Joaquin, and Leon municipalities, with no automatic city overnight-base mapping. - Batch 3: 22 Boracay candidates appended (16 accepted, three rejected, three unresolved). All accepted places are on Boracay Island, in the actual municipality of Malay, province of Aklan. Mount Luho is unresolved, never accepted; Spider House and the offshore Crystal Cove candidate also remain unresolved. Nonie’s, Giuseppe, and Little Taj use operator-supplied addresses; hotel/estate permissions remain independent unknowns.

- Batch 2: 22 Bohol candidates appended (18 accepted, three rejected, one unresolved). Panglao and Dauis are explicitly separated. Mainland Carmen, Baclayon, Loboc, and Tagbilaran City do not automatically inherit the Panglao overnight base. The National Museum street address is sourced; unknown addresses remain null. Shaka’s conflicting street-address listings are not promoted to a verified address; Waze corroborates the actual Panglao municipality. Pamilacan is a physical island/barangay under Baclayon, not a boat package.

- Batch 1: 25 Cebu accepted records saved as valid JSON. Cebu City venues retain matching catalog IDs; Badian, Oslob, Alcoy, Carmen, Boljoon, Bantayan, Sibonga, Balamban, Lapu-Lapu City, and Carcar City are recorded under their actual municipalities. Outlying directory records receive no automatic Cebu City overnight-base mapping. Full street addresses confirmed only for National Museum Cebu, TOPS, and Cebu Ocean Park. Other addresses remain unknown.
