# Supplying a photograph later

No owners were contacted, no permission requests were sent, and no upload system was added. The remaining checklist is a handoff for a maintainer, not a request that has already been sent or approved.

For each photograph, provide:

1. **Place ID:** use the stable ID in `needs-supplied-photos.json`; also identify the exact branch, municipality and subject in the image.
2. **Image file:** the original or highest available quality file, preferably at least 1200 pixels wide. Do not send a screenshot, search thumbnail, AI image or a neighboring attraction's image.
3. **Photographer/rights holder:** identify who took the photo and who can authorize reuse. A venue owner does not necessarily own a contractor's or visitor's photograph.
4. **Permission:** a dated written grant covering publication on Lakbay's public website, hosting optimized local copies, and necessary resizing, compression and cropping for cards and detail pages. State any restrictions. A public social-media post, a media-kit download or a supplied file alone does not establish permission.
5. **Attribution preference:** the exact credit name and any required link, or an explicit statement that no credit is required. Retain the rights holder even where public attribution is waived.

A useful permission statement is: “I am the photographer or authorized rights holder for the attached image of [place and branch, place ID]. I permit Lakbay to publish it on its public website, host optimized copies, and resize, compress and crop it for responsive display. Credit as [name/link, or no public credit requested]. Restrictions, if any: [details].” Record the date and the actual grant; this template is not permission by itself.

Keep the permission evidence with the project’s research records, with access appropriate to the sender's consent. Do not publish private correspondence or contact details just to create a credit. For an existing open-license photo, retain the individual image page, photographer, original download URL and exact license URL as well as the supplied file.

A maintainer must then visually check the subject and exact location, review the permission, use the existing Sharp optimization helper, and add the full photo metadata to that place's editorial record. Use meaningful alt text and a source/permission reference suitable for publication. Record all modifications and preserve attribution. Run image/data checks and regenerate exports and image credits. The current automated photo-only publisher accepts reviewed Commons/Flickr licenses; a private permission record needs a separately reviewed maintainer update, not a fabricated Commons/Flickr source URL.

Some gaps have plausible open-license Flickr candidates whose image files could not be retrieved for visual review. An accessible copy from the same credited source may resolve those gaps without a new owner photograph, provided the source license and exact subject are checked before publication.
