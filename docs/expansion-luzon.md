# Luzon expansion review

Checked 21 October 2026. Existing directory candidates and research IDs were compared before selection. This file adds selected records without editing the original 200. Unknown coordinates, admission, hours, and accessibility are not invented. Duration values and transport buffers are editorial estimates.

## Incremental batches

1. Manila: 12 accepted records saved. Three editorial omissions are promoted using their original IDs: Museo de Intramuros, Destileria Limtuaco Museum, Bahay Tsinoy. They were previously omitted for geographic breadth, not closed. Baluarte uses its existing research ID. All four are supported by the [Intramuros Administration directory](https://intramuros.gov.ph/sites/). New restaurant records use current first-party visitor pages from [Eng Bee Tin](https://www.engbeetin.com/), [Ilustrado](https://ilustradorestaurant.ph/), and [Barbara's](https://www.barbaras.ph/contact-us). Only identity and location were adopted from Barbara's; placeholder contact text was ignored.

[Manila city](https://manila.gov.ph/category/parks-development/page/6/) supports Arroceros. [Quezon City](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/place/ninoy-aquino-parks-and-wildlife-center/) supports the wildlife park; [Ateneo](https://ateneoartgallery.com/contact) supports its art gallery's address and campus visitor restrictions. Both QC entries are manual, have no existing overnight base, and do not imply a connection-day decision. [Museo Pambata](https://www.museopambata.com.ph/) publishes current visitor information, and [Manila Ocean Park](https://manilaoceanpark.com/) carries 2026 updates. No sub-attraction within these museums or parks is counted separately.

2. Baguio: 14 accepted additions saved (26 cumulative). The [official city directory](https://infoboard.baguio.gov.ph/tourists-spots) supports the seven named local cultural, retail, chapel, and park venues. [TPB](https://rtf.tpb.gov.ph/cordillera/baguio/) supports Mines View. First-party restaurant pages establish the specific venues: [Original Good Taste](https://goodtaste.ph/) is Lapu Lapu, not Otek; [Batirol](https://www.chocolatedebatirol.com/blank-3/) is the Camp John Hay garden venue; [Casa Vallejo](https://www.casavallejo.online/) identifies Hill Station; [Amare](https://amarelacucina.com/contact-amare-la-cucina/) identifies Outlook; [Baguio Craft Brewery](https://www.baguiocraftbrewery.net/) and [Craft 1945](https://craft1945.com/) are different businesses and locations. Cafe 1945 is folded into its Craft 1945 parent instead of counted again. Baguio remains a province-null highly urbanized city. Each unverified neighborhood uses a venue-specific cluster; the explicitly located Camp John Hay cafe uses the existing John Hay cluster.

3. Batanes: 18 accepted additions saved (44 cumulative). Municipality labels come from the [provincial tourism map](https://breathtakingbatanes.com/maps/), [DOT's municipal guide](https://www.region2fun.ph/official/batanes-2/), [provincial portal](https://bookings.breathtakingbatanes.com/), and [Sabtang attraction page](https://breathtakingbatanes.com/tourist-attraction-6/). The [National Museum contact page](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/contact-us/) specifically places its Batanes venue at Old Loran, Itbud, Uyugan. The map's live page renders a shell, but current indexed official text exposes the named municipal assignments. No point was inferred from its map.

Four Basco food venues are listed under the town-proper heading in the province's currently hosted [food map](https://breathtakingbatanes.com/foodmap.pdf). The separate Casa Napoli main branch is not added again. Street-level addresses, hours, and menu prices remain unknown; the map is evidence of identity and Basco location, not a guarantee of service on a particular day. Honesty Shop has independent DOT evidence in Ivana.

Road outings outside Basco receive a full-day allowance, not a claim that one stop takes eight hours. The [provincial tourism site](https://breathtakingbatanes.com/) advertises South Batan and Sabtang routes, and [LetsGoBatanes](https://www.letsgobatanes.com/) describes the road/boat connection and village stops. Sabtang entries are explicit manual full-day excursions from the existing Basco base: they require a confirmed transfer, return boat, and operator arrangements. They do not create an overnight base or assert a crossing is available or safe. Morong Beach and its natural arch are one record, not two.
