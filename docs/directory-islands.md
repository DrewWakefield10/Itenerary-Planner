# Directory research — Palawan, Siargao, Davao

Review date: 21 October 2026. Only identity and actual municipality determine acceptance. Missing street addresses, barangays, and pins remain null. Existing checked facts retain their original dates; a new review never advances unrelated coordinate dates.

## Progress batches

### Batch 1 — Palawan, candidates 1–25

13 El Nido places and 12 Puerto Princesa places accepted. Sources include TPB destination itineraries, PCSD municipal tourism inventory, and Puerto Princesa City's attraction inventory. Package products and generic breaks do not count. Offshore places remain individual directory records; this does not authorize individual scheduling beside their package. Existing itinerary stop IDs are retained.

Puerto Princesa City is geographically grouped with Palawan; it is an independent highly urbanized city, not administratively under the province. Province is retained as geographic directory grouping. Source dates 18/19 October are prior identity/pin reviews, while municipality review dates are 21 October. Duli's Barangay Bucana/Sitio Duli address is from the venue contact page. Other unconfirmed barangays and addresses are null.

Pin evidence remains field-specific and retains its prior estimate/unverified status. Null pins must not be replaced with city-center coordinates. No opening, admission-price, transport availability, or weather-safety claim is made by directory acceptance.

### Batch 2 — Palawan 26–35 and Siargao 36–45

Seven Coron places plus Long Beach and Port Barton Beach in San Vicente and Fort Santa Isabel in Taytay complete 35 accepted Palawan places. The three San Vicente/Taytay records have no itinerary base or catalog-stop mapping. Fort Santa Isabel's Rizal Street / Poblacion address is directly supported by NHCP. Lualhati Park is Poblacion 2 in the Coron municipal report. Kayangan and Barracuda are in Coron municipality according to the biodiversity wetlands inventory, not simply because tours leave Coron town.

Ten Siargao records have actual municipalities recorded: General Luna (Cloud 9, Catangnan–Cabitoonan Bridge, Naked, Daku, Guyam), Pilar (Magpupungko, Tayangban, Maasin), Del Carmen (Sugba Lagoon, Barangay Caub), and San Isidro (Pacifico). PNA's Pilar municipal destination list and DPWH records support Maasin's Pilar location, correcting travel-site Dapa attributions. Historical closure articles only establish identity/location here; their old operating or pandemic rules are not reused.

### Batch 3 — Siargao 46–53 and Davao 54–68

Siargao reaches 18 accepted places across General Luna, Pilar, Del Carmen, San Isidro, Santa Monica, San Benito, Dapa, and Socorro. New directory-only records are Del Carmen Mangrove Viewdeck, Poneas Lake, Sohoton Cove, Corregidor Island, and Paghungawan Marsh. The first four are explicitly assigned to their actual municipalities in the DENR-hosted July 2023 ecotourism list; Caraga State University's wetland study explicitly locates Paghungawan Marsh in Barangay Jaboy, Pilar. Sohoton is on Bucas Grande in Socorro, not on the main Siargao island. These five records have no existing catalog-stop mapping, and no itinerary base is inferred. Directory inclusion is not permission to enter a protected wetland, launch a boat, or approach wildlife.

15 Davao places accepted, all in Davao City. Province is null because the city is a highly urbanized city, independent of any province; region is Davao Region. The city's Baguio District is not Baguio City. Eden's Toril resort address is separate from its Matina reservations office, and Malagos Garden Resort is separate from the Agdao corporate office. Barangays and partial addresses are recorded only where the venue/city states them explicitly.

Museo Dabawenyo uses the existing stable ID `davao-museo-dabawenyo-area`, with the directory name Museo Dabawenyo Event Center and the city-reported Mabini Street / Barangay 10-A address. Its unknown new entrance pin stays null. It is distinct from NMP-Davao at People's Park Compound and from Davao Museum of History and Ethnography. The NMP webpage's fifth-floor Museo reference is not used to overwrite the city's May 2025 venue announcement. Current exhibitions and access remain unverified.

### Batch 4 — Final audit candidates 69–86

Ten existing package products rejected as directory places: four El Nido boat-day routes, five Coron boat-day routes, and one Siargao tri-island route. Six existing generic activities rejected: El Nido town walk, Local market & lunch, Coron town lunch, Puerto Princesa city lunch, Davao local food break, and Catangnan coastal afternoon. They remain usable historical itinerary snapshots where supported, but do not inflate distinct physical-place counts. These are actual reviewed catalog candidates rather than fabricated research entries.

Two real island identities remain unresolved for municipality: Malcapuya Island and Banana Island. The [Coron 2023 municipal report](https://coron.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Accomplishment-Report-CY-2023.pdf) discusses claimed/disputed Malcapuya land while the [TPB 2025 itinerary](https://www.tpb.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PBD_PR-No.-2025.07.049_taiwan-agent_ipd-1.pdf) labels the Malcapuya route a Culion Island Escapade. The [PCSD marine habitat report](https://pcsd.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Palawan-DB-report-2024-FINAL.pdf) shows municipal boundaries in the area, but no definitive uncontested administration was established from the reviewed text. Banana's operator listing establishes the named island but not its municipality. Both are excluded from the public accepted count, retain their catalog-stop ID for audit, and have null municipality/base and unverified municipality metadata. Neither gets assigned to Coron merely because tours depart there.
