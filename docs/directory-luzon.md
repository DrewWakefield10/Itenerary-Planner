# Directory research - Luzon

Review date: 21 October 2026. This directory is separate from itinerary generation. Accepted means identity and actual municipality are supported, not that access, prices, opening hours, or safety are confirmed. Unknown fields stay null. Existing coordinate reviews keep their previous dates and statuses.

## Batch log

1. Baguio: 20 candidates saved; 16 accepted, 3 rejected, 1 unresolved. [Official city directory](https://infoboard.baguio.gov.ph/tourists-spots) was re-reviewed through indexed official text today; its JavaScript page does not expose the directory to the text renderer. Bell Church is correctly assigned to La Trinidad and excluded from the Baguio-city shortlist. Loakan is a transport facility, and the mall was not selected. PMA public access is unresolved.

## Administrative geography

Baguio is a highly urbanized city and is recorded with province=null rather than administratively placing it under Benguet. The [PSA 2024 Cordillera census release](https://rssocar.psa.gov.ph/content/highlights-cordillera-2024-census-population-results-2024-census-population) separates Baguio from Benguet and confirms its CAR region. Postal references to Benguet in attraction addresses do not change that administrative treatment. Sagada is a municipality of Mountain Province ([PSA PSGC](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/citimuni/1404400000)).

2. Sagada: 14 candidates saved; 10 accepted, 1 rejected, 3 unresolved. [Municipal iPass](https://umali-kayo.sagada.gov.ph/) was opened today and supports destination identity in Sagada. The [church reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_Saint_Mary_the_Virgin_(Sagada)) separately supports its exact identity and municipality. Street/barangay details are null when unavailable. A trail overlapping Ampacao was excluded, and less-established routes remain unresolved rather than forced into the selected list. Accepted cave/trail identity does not confirm current access.

3. Batanes: 15 candidates saved; 12 accepted, 1 rejected, 2 unresolved. Today's review of the [official provincial map indexed directory](https://breathtakingbatanes.com/maps/) explicitly assigns sites to Basco, Mahatao, and Ivana. Its live text renderer exposes only the shell; indexed official directory text provides municipality labels. [DOT Region 2](https://www.region2fun.ph/official/batanes-2/) independently supplies municipality headings and [regional context](https://www.region2fun.ph/official/about-cagayan-valley-and-the-northern-philippine-islands/). [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Basco) supplies Basco Plaza's town listing. Mahatao and Ivana directory places have itinerary_base_slug=null, even if the existing catalog offers them as outings: a browse group is not an administrative town or an automatic overnight-base assignment. Exact street addresses are mostly unknown.

4. Manila / Metro Manila: 23 candidates saved; 20 accepted, 3 rejected by curation. The accepted list spans Manila, Makati, Pasig, San Juan, Quezon City, Marikina, Las Pinas, and Taguig. The three extra Intramuros museums are real, but were omitted from the fixed shortlist to make room for geographic breadth. Administrative city names always describe the physical venue, not the broad Manila browse group. New places outside Manila city have no itinerary base or stop mapping.

## Manila source record

- The three [National Museum pages](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/our-museums/national-museum-of-fine-arts/) were opened individually, together with the [official central-complex directory](https://reservation.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/reservation/book/region-museum). Museum-specific street addresses remain null where only the complex location was reviewed.
- [Intramuros official sites](https://intramuros.gov.ph/sites/), [Fort Santiago](https://intramuros.gov.ph/fs/), [Casa Manila](https://intramuros.gov.ph/cmm/), [museum address directory](https://intramuros.gov.ph/sites-museums/), and [Centro launch announcement](https://intramuros.gov.ph/2024/06/09/intramuros-unveils-centro-de-turismo-a-gateway-to-the-past-present-and-future/) support identity and the Manila district relationship. No generic footer address was substituted for an attraction's address.
- [NPDC Paco Park](https://npdc.gov.ph/paco-park/) and [Rizal Park](https://npdc.gov.ph/rizal-park/) support the two park entries.
- [Ayala Museum](https://www.ayalamuseum.org/visit) and [Yuchengco Museum](https://yuchengcomuseum.org/visit/visitor-information/) publish their Makati venue addresses.
- [Pasig's official visitor announcement](https://pasigcity.gov.ph/news-and-releases/free-guided-tour-sa-pasig-city-museum-991) gives Plaza Rizal, Barangay San Jose. Its old promotion is not imported as a current offer.
- [NHCP El Deposito](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/el-deposito/) and [Pinaglabanan registry](https://philhistoricsites.nhcp.gov.ph/registry_database/pang-alaalang-dambana-ng-pinaglabanan/) establish two distinct San Juan museums rather than one duplicate entry for their shared surroundings.
- [Quezon City shrine page](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/place/quezon-memorial-shrine-museo-ni-manuel-quezon/) and [city-library Art in Island profile](https://qcpl.quezoncity.gov.ph/article/p0dpurpscoqaz4agdffagnq5) provide separate venue addresses and barangays. No neighboring park or museum interior is counted twice.
- [Marikina's tourism ordinance](https://www.marikina.gov.ph/ordinance/ORDINANCE%20NO.%2065%20S.%202015.pdf) supports the city-owned shoe museum identity; the exact street address remains null. The old ordinance is not evidence of a live fee.
- [Las Pinas city heritage page](https://laspinascity.gov.ph/lifestyle/18/index) supplies the church/organ location. The instrument and church are one directory place.
- [The Mind Museum](https://www.themindmuseum.org/visit-us/plan-your-visit) confirms its physical location in Taguig, not Manila city.
- [PSA NCR city list](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/cities/1300000000) and [PSGC summary](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/summary) confirm the region's cities and the lack of a province subdivision for these cities. Manila-group entries therefore have province=null.

## Final coverage and limitations

72 candidates were reviewed: 58 accepted, 8 rejected, and 6 unresolved. Accepted totals: Manila 20, Baguio 16, Batanes 12, Sagada 10. Unknown exact addresses and barangays remain null. Existing coordinates are copied only with their previous metadata; source review of identity and municipality on October 21 does not refresh or upgrade a pin. Access to private venues, religious observances, museum closures, mountain routes, and guide arrangements must still be confirmed separately. No fees or opening hours are asserted by this directory schema. Rejected/unresolved candidates are audit records and must stay out of public results.
