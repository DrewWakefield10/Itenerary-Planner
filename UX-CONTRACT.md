# Lakbay directory behavior

The geographic brief (21 October 2026, 11 browsing groups) remains authoritative for locations. The account brief and the user's Google-only clarification (22 October 2026) govern personal saving: no ChatGPT account or separate password is required. `docs/accounts.md`, `lib/auth.ts`, `lib/account-api.ts` and `lib/account-store.ts` define identity, data lifecycle, ownership, idempotency and revision policy. No billing or public account-trip sharing is introduced.

## Flow ledger

- Navigation is Discover, Explore, Plan a Trip, My Trips and Saved Places, followed by the existing account entry (Sign in with Google when enabled and signed out). Explore is active on destination and advice pages. Footer links lead to Explore and its Travel advice view. Authentication readiness and ownership remain unchanged.
- `/explore` contains 13 independently linkable overnight-base guides covering 11 browsing groups, grouped visually for Palawan. Interests require supported catalog places; legacy Budget/Family/Hidden Gems URLs remain planning lenses. Search, interest and favorite filters compose in the URL and restore with Back/Forward. `/explore/advice` retains all eight former demo starters and four general planning checklists. A small advice preview also appears beneath destinations.
- Existing `/destinations/[slug]` URLs retain their overview and fact-level source metadata, estimates and dates, with three relevant Discover cards and up to two food links. See all places uses the correct `destination` query filter. Palawan's legacy page stays explicitly ambiguous and offers the three real bases, rather than silently routing a trip to El Nido. General preparation articles are not represented as destination-specific verification.
- `/templates` permanently redirects to `/explore/advice`, retaining query values and the browser's fragment. `/explore?view=advice` is also a compatibility redirect to that index. The two views use distinct routes so production prefetching preserves correct page titles. Existing article detail routes did not exist; new articles use `/explore/advice/{transport,budgeting,packing,preparation}`. Unknown article slugs have an app-owned not-found message and a relevant return link. Catch-all route metadata owns document titles, including the advice view and destination pages.
- Destination and starter actions open the separate `/planner` with supported prefill parameters. A fresh planner accepts valid input; an existing differing draft gets the shared native confirmation dialog. Replace/add choices change only destinations, and suggested duration requires its own opt-in. Keep my draft or Escape preserves storage. Applied/kept requests are consumed synchronously so refresh cannot replay them. All existing base-count and scheduling constraints still validate. Malformed or unreadable drafts are not overwritten without an explicit new-draft action. Saved itinerary snapshots and bookmarks are never part of this operation.

- Discover destination tabs: select a destination button or filter; update the same combined result set. Explore Destinations buttons within Discover also scroll to Spots to discover after updating results, retaining keyboard focus and respecting reduced motion. URL restoration and ordinary filter edits do not trigger this scroll. Category, interest, name/description/municipality search compose with it. Filters and page are URL-restorable; filter edits reset pagination. Empty results offer Clear all filters.
- Food discovery uses the same labeled native controls, with café, dining, bakery, dessert, breakfast and food-market filters. A venue has one primary counting category and may have several supported roles. Café experience does not automatically prove coffee or full-meal suitability. Exact coffee searches require an explicit coffee role; named meals replace flexible meal placeholders and share the daily allowance. Source review, operation evidence, hours and missing pins remain independent. Saved activity snapshots are never hydrated from updated food profiles.
- Browse details: cards link to stable `/discover/[id]`; real city/municipality and administrative address are separate from browsing group. Legacy catalog links remain readable but are not counted as accepted directory entries.
- Editorial place content and media live in separate stable-ID overlays. They may improve directory display/search/exports, but must never regenerate or rewrite saved trip snapshots or bookmark records. Photos carry original/source/license/creator metadata; metadata-only video checks are explicitly distinguished from footage review. Video sections are absent without an available, place-matched record. Practical unknowns stay unknown; itinerary budget allowances and estimated durations remain labeled as estimates.
- View map: only a supplied point is plotted. Missing coordinates are stated. Nearby requires a compatible local overnight base and verified pins; no cross-destination nearby lists or inferred routes.
- Add a place: only reviewed compatible stop mappings enable the existing dialog. Preview does not mutate storage. Confirm & add rebuilds validation against the current saved trip. Failure leaves the trip and user's choices intact; cancel/Escape does not save. Never silently choose a different base.
- Download: static CSV and JSON exports include all accepted records and their sources. Candidate decisions and progress are separate exports.

## State and accessibility

Instagram is an optional stable-ID media overlay for every place category. No script, widget or request is made before Load Instagram posts. Source links and review limitations remain accessible without loading. Script failure offers an explicit retry; a narrow screen offers links. Third-party rendering does not establish current visiting conditions or image reuse rights. Hide the entire section when no approved resource exists. A successful script download alone is never recorded as a confirmed individual embed.

Detail covers prefer the existing approved photograph. Only its absence permits the first reviewed, embeddable original Instagram post to appear as a cover preview, behind Load Instagram preview. If neither resource exists, keep the existing art. A failed approved-photo download does not reclassify its license or silently load Instagram. The selected post appears once, with a visible View original on Instagram link and unaltered official widget controls. Other posts below remain separately opt-in. Controls stay disabled until hydration can handle activation. Embeds never count as approved photographs, and nothing rewrites saved itineraries or bookmarks.

Use real buttons and links, visible focus, labeled native selects, and text result counts in a status region. Filters are local and synchronous; remote readership loading/failure does not block the directory. Its existing abortable request and freshness rules remain separate from catalog acceptance. Dialogs use the established native HTML dialog surface, accessible title, focus containment and restoration; browser alert/confirm/prompt are not used. English UI, peso amounts, and original local names are preserved. Reduced motion follows global styles.
